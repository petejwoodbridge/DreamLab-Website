Questions

AGENTIC, SOVEREIGN
& EVERYTHING ELSE

What is agentic AI, and how can dreamlab help?

Agentic AI systems don't just answer questions — they plan, use tools and take actions to complete multi-step work, such as researching a market, processing documents or running a production pipeline. dreamlab designs, builds and runs agentic systems for studios, enterprises and public sector teams across the UK, from single task agents to orchestrated teams of specialist agents.

What is an agent harness?

An agent harness is everything around the AI model that makes an agent reliable: the tools it can use, its memory and context, permissions, feedback loops, evals and guardrails. The model provides the intelligence; the harness makes it dependable in production. Harness engineering is where most of the work — and most of the value — in a real agent sits, and it's a core part of what we build.

What is sovereign AI?

Sovereign AI means running AI on infrastructure you control — typically open-weight models hosted on your own servers, in a UK data centre or fully air-gapped — so your data, IP and models never pass through a third-party AI provider. It suits regulated industries, public sector bodies and any organisation whose data is too sensitive or valuable to send elsewhere.

Can you build AI on our company data without it leaving our control?

Yes. We build private knowledge bases and knowledge graphs from your documents, data and know-how, and connect them to sovereign or privately hosted models. Access controls, audit trails and GDPR-aligned governance are designed in from the start, so your people and your agents get the full context of your business while your company data stays safe and under your control.

What is a knowledge graph, and why does it matter for AI?

A knowledge graph is a connected, machine-readable map of your organisation — the people, projects, clients, products and ideas, and how they relate. Grounding AI in a knowledge graph (GraphRAG) gives far more accurate, explainable answers than searching documents alone, with sources the AI can cite.

Can you help us write funding bids and tenders?

Yes. We support grant applications (Innovate UK, UKRI, Heritage Fund, Arts Council), investment business cases, and commercial and public sector tenders — from bid strategy and technical writing through to review and compliance checks. We also built Bearings, a free funding finder for North West businesses.

Where is dreamlab based?

dreamlab has labs at MediaCity in Manchester, in Liverpool, in the Lake District (Cumbria) and in London, and works with organisations across the UK and internationally.
